Frequently Asked Questions

Quick clarifications regarding shipping, materials, and warranty.

What materials do you use in LAILA BAGS?

We exclusively select 100% full-grain Italian calf leather, supple lambskin nappa, and hand-buffed water-resistant suede sourced directly from certified tanneries in Tuscany and Portugal. Our hardware is electroplated in 18k light-gold with anti-tarnish protective coatings.

How long does standard shipping take?

Domestic shipments arrive in 2-4 business days. International express shipments dispatched via DHL arrive within 3-5 business days. All orders over $150 qualify for complimentary shipping.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer 30-day risk-free returns and exchanges on all unworn items in their original packaging with protective tags intact. We supply prepaid shipping return labels.
