Our Humble Beginnings

The Story Behind Every Stitch

LAILA BAGS was founded with a singular conviction: luxury should not be defined by synthetic prestige or hyper-inflated markups, but by the tangible soul of noble raw materials and master artisan craftsmanship.

Beginning in a small Tuscan workshop in 2021, our design team collaborated directly with multi-generational leather tanners in Santa Croce sull'Arno. Every cut of full-grain leather is naturally vegetable-tanned, ensuring that each handbag develops a distinct, gorgeous patina that matures alongside its owner.
